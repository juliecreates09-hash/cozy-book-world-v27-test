Browned Butter Shortbread

Tender, buttery shortbread rounds.

Let’s bake shortbread

  1. 1. Brown the butter

    Gently brown the butter as described in the original recipe, then allow it to cool completely.

  2. 2. Make the dough

    Combine the cooled butter with the sugar, vanillin sugar, salt, and milk. Add the flour and baking powder.

  3. 3. Shape logs

    Shape the dough into even logs and chill thoroughly.

  4. 4. Slice

    Cut the logs into thin rounds and place them apart on parchment paper.

  5. 5. Bake

    Bake on the middle rack at 180°C (356°F), conventional heat, for about 15 minutes.
