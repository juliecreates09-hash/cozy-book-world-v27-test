General information

This privacy policy explains which personal data may be processed when you visit Crumbles’ Christmas Bakery, why this happens, and what rights you have.

Hosting

Crumbles’ Christmas Bakery is delivered using Cloudflare Workers. When you access the website, Cloudflare and network providers may process technical connection data such as IP address, request time, requested URL and browser information to deliver the site, maintain security and investigate technical issues. Where the website operator is responsible for this processing, the legal basis is legitimate interests in secure and reliable website operation under Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Processing outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States, may occur. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy for further information.

Encrypted connections

When published over HTTPS, traffic between your browser and the server is encrypted. This does not apply when opening local test files.

Cookies and local functions

According to the current implementation, we do not set our own cookies for personalized advertising or profiling. Ingredient calculators, print features and checklists operate in your browser. If technologies requiring consent are introduced, this policy will be updated and consent obtained where required.

Website analytics

No standalone Cloudflare Web Analytics script is included in the HTML files of this website package. Whether additional analytics are enabled at the Cloudflare hosting level depends on the account settings. If such services are activated, this policy must be updated accordingly.

Fonts

This test version uses system or locally available fonts. It does not request fonts from Google Fonts.

Amazon book links

The website may link to book listings on Amazon. You leave our website when you click a link, and the destination provider’s privacy policy applies. Purchases are not made through this website.

Pinterest and other external links

Links may lead to Pinterest and other third-party sites. Their privacy policies apply once you follow a link. The current implementation does not plan to embed Pinterest content or use the Pinterest Tag. Pinterest privacy policy.

Email contact

If you contact us by email, we process your email address, message and any information you voluntarily provide to respond. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) or (b) GDPR, depending on the context. Data is retained only as needed to respond or comply with legal retention duties. Email service providers may also process data.

Recipients and international transfers

Hosting, analytics and email service providers may receive personal data. Third-party sites process data under their own responsibility when you follow links. Transfers outside the European Economic Area may occur, subject to the applicable provider agreements and safeguards.

Retention and required data

Personal data is retained only as long as necessary for its purpose or legal obligations. Service providers determine retention of their technical data. Technical connection data is required to access the website; otherwise you are generally not required to provide personal information.

Automated decisions

We do not use automated decision-making, including profiling, within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR.

Your rights

Subject to legal requirements, you have rights of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), notification (Art. 19), portability (Art. 20), objection (Art. 21), withdrawal of consent (Art. 7(3)), and complaint to a supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). Contact us using the email address above.

Right to object

Where processing relies on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, you may object on grounds relating to your particular situation. Processing will cease unless compelling legitimate grounds or legal claims justify it.

Supervisory authority

You may complain to a data protection authority, including the Brandenburg Commissioner for Data Protection and Access to Information: www.lda.brandenburg.de.

Changes

We update this policy if the website’s services, processing activities or legal requirements change.
