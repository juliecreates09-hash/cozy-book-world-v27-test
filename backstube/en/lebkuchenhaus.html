Crumbles’ Magical Gingerbread House

Bake, assemble, and decorate a festive gingerbread house together.

⏱ Allow at least 2 hours to chill · 180 °C (356 °F) · ~15 min per tray · assembly and drying time extra🏠 House base: 24 × 21 cm

From dough to a festive little house — one step at a time.

What you need

  • Honey — 200 g
  • Sugar — 100 g
  • Vanilla-flavored sugar (vanillin sugar) — 1 packet (packet size varies; check product)
  • Butter or margarine — 125 g
  • Wheat flour — 560 g
  • Baking powder — 4 level tsp
  • Christmas baking spice/flavoring — 1 packet (use the original recipe product; quantity needs verification)
  • Medium eggs — 2
  • Milk for brushing — approx. 2 tbsp
  • Powdered sugar for royal icing — approx. 250 g
  • Medium egg white for royal icing — approx. 1
  • Decorating icing, sprinkles, chocolate candies, and powdered sugar — as desired

Bake your gingerbread house

01 · Prepare the dough
  1. Prepare the honey mixture

    Warm the honey, sugar, vanillin sugar, and butter or margarine while stirring until combined. Let cool.

  2. Make and chill the dough

    Combine the flour and baking powder. Knead with the Christmas flavoring, eggs, and cooled honey mixture. Cover and refrigerate for about two hours.

02 · Bake the pieces
  1. Prepare the oven and templates

    Preheat the oven to 180°C (356°F), conventional heat. Line baking sheets with parchment paper. Prepare templates for the walls and roof.

  2. Cut out the house pieces

    Roll out portions of dough to about 5 mm thick. Cut out walls and roof pieces using the templates. Cut out a door and windows if desired.

  3. Bake the pieces

    Brush lightly with milk and bake for approximately 15 minutes per tray. Allow to cool completely.

03 · Build and decorate
  1. Bake the base

    Roll out the remaining dough and cut a rectangular base measuring 24 × 21 cm (about 9.4 × 8.3 in), using the two joined A4 template sections. Bake and cool completely.

  2. Assemble the house

    Sift the powdered sugar and mix with the egg white to make very stiff icing. Assemble the walls on the base, then attach the roof. Support the pieces until the icing holds.

  3. Decorate for Christmas

    Once the house is stable, decorate with icing, writing icing, sprinkles, and chocolate candies. Dust with powdered sugar if desired and allow everything to dry thoroughly.

Templates & assembly

Let the baked walls and roof pieces cool completely. Check the printed template dimensions before assembly. Pipe a thick line of royal icing along the wall edges, join the walls on the base, and support them until firm. Attach the roof only after the walls hold securely; let the icing set before decorating.

Print the templates at 100% scale. Check the 50 mm calibration line before cutting.

Baking safely together

Adults handle the hot honey mixture, oven, sharp tools, and assembly. Do not eat raw dough. Prefer pasteurized egg white for icing and check that it sets firmly.

The fit of the templates and the stability of the finished house have not yet been tested in practice. This remains a test version.
