Christmas family activity

Crumbles’ Cozy Family Baking Afternoon

A relaxed plan for baking together at Christmas.

Plan your time: Around 2–3 hours, depending on your chosen recipe

What you need

  • One complete cookie recipe and its ingredients
  • Baking trays, baking paper and suitable utensils
  • Aprons, drinks and a clean work surface
  • Optional cookie cutters and decorations

Let’s make it together

  1. Choose a recipe first and check its preparation, chilling and baking times.
  2. Share age-appropriate jobs: measuring, mixing, cutting out shapes or decorating.
  3. Wash hands, clear the table and measure ingredients using the chosen recipe.
  4. Bake together, following that recipe exactly. Adults handle the oven, hot trays and sharp utensils.
  5. Plan a little game, story or drink break while dough chills or cookies bake.
  6. Let the cookies cool, decorate if suitable, then tidy up together.

Crumbles’ little idea

Make a family baking team: one ingredient helper, one shape maker and one Christmas music chooser.

For a safe family activity

Follow the recipe’s allergen and hygiene guidance. Adults must supervise all heating, cutting and baking tasks.
