Christmas family activity

Decorate Cookies Together

A colorful afternoon decorating cookies with Crumbles.

Plan your time: 60–90 minutes, plus drying time

What you need

  • Fully baked and cooled cookies
  • Icing or suitable decorating pens
  • Sprinkles, small bowls, teaspoons and baking paper

Let’s make it together

  1. Cover the table with baking paper and arrange the decorations in small bowls.
  2. Sort the cooled cookies by shape. Everyone chooses a few stars, hearts or trees.
  3. Spoon a little icing into small bowls. Decorate the cookies, then add sprinkles while the icing is still wet.
  4. Lay the decorated cookies flat on baking paper and allow the icing to dry completely.
  5. Admire your cookie gallery together and store the cookies carefully once dry.

Crumbles’ little idea

Invite everyone to decorate one special Christmas star. Put them together as a festive star gallery.

For a safe family activity

Young children do best with large cookies and just a few toppings. Small hard sugar pearls can be a choking hazard. Check icing and sprinkle labels for allergens.
