Assembly guide

  1. Print the nine A4 pages at actual size (100%), not “fit to page”; measure the 50 mm test line.
  2. Cut out the templates. You need front and back walls, two side walls, and two roof panels. Door and windows are optional.
  3. Use the recipe’s 24 × 21 cm rectangular base; join the two A4 base-template sections at 100% scale.
  4. Let all baked pieces cool completely. Dry-fit the walls and roof without icing first.
  5. Prepare very stiff icing. Attach the front wall and one side wall to the base and support them. Add the remaining walls.
  6. Allow the wall joints to set before adding the roof. Support both roof panels until secure.
  7. Decorate only when the structure is stable; let dry thoroughly.

Adults handle the hot honey mixture, oven, sharp tools, and assembly. Do not eat raw dough. Prefer pasteurized egg white for icing and check that it sets firmly.

The fit of the templates and the stability of the finished house have not yet been tested in practice. This remains a test version.
