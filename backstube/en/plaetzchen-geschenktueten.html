Christmas Cookie Gift Bags

Little homemade Christmas gifts from Crumbles’ bakery.

Plan your time: 30–45 minutes, plus cooling and drying

What you need

  • Fully cooled, dry cookies
  • Food-safe gift bags or clean tins
  • Ribbon, paper gift tags and pens
  • Optional stickers

Let’s make it together

  1. Choose cookies that have cooled completely. Iced cookies must also be fully dry.
  2. Arrange a few cookies in each food-safe bag or clean tin without crushing them.
  3. Make a gift tag with the recipient’s name and a warm Christmas greeting.
  4. Add the cookie type, preparation date and relevant ingredients or allergens for the recipient.
  5. Close the packages and store them according to the cookie recipe until gifting.

Crumbles’ little idea

Write “Baked for you with a little Christmas magic!” on each tag.

For a safe family activity

Use food-safe packaging. Keep cookies with different allergens separate, and check the relevant recipe for storage guidance.
